## Claims corrected

These were asserted — two of them in this documentation, the rest during the session — and
were wrong.

**"A constant per-MWh strike cannot reorder the exercise days."** True only at a zero rate.
Undiscounted the cost is `Σ (P − K)·q` and the `K` leg is a constant times a fixed volume,
so it cannot reorder anything. Discounted it is `Σ DF·(P − K)·q`, and `−K·Σ DF·q` depends on
*when* you exercise. `MODEL-CONVENTIONS.md` stated it without the condition, and §2 of the
notebook still carried the uncorrected version two commits after the doc was fixed — a
correction that does not propagate is half a correction.

**That a strike leaves the day-selection gain unchanged at a positive rate.** A test written
to assert it failed, and the model was right: 4.6516 struck against 4.5814 unstruck.

**That the value is concave in the strike at 10 %.** The residual sign was read backwards. A
convex function lies *below* its chord, and the slopes rise 8,761 → 8,891, so it is convex.

**That charging financing on the gas needs a new model input.** It does not. The optimiser
never sees `F`, it sees `DF·F`; a forward curve in contango at the discount rate flattens
that, and the timing gain vanishes exactly. A flat curve beside a positive rate is
internally inconsistent, and the "free" financing gain is the model reporting that
inconsistency faithfully.

## Behaviour established

Measured, and now asserted in `tests/`.

- **Financing is interest, not an artefact.** The deterministic schedule and the flat
  benchmark move identical gas at identical prices — nominal totals equal to `0.00e+00` —
  and the balance between them peaks at **97,260.27 EUR on 2027-12-21**. The PV of interest
  on it is **4,192.93 EUR** against **4,192.36** reported, a 0.01 % gap.
- **Contango at the discount rate zeroes the timing gain.** `DF·F` flat to **1.4e-14**,
  intrinsic exactly `0.000e+00`, and its two halves large and equal-and-opposite. Extrinsic
  untouched at 2.6708, because optionality comes from volatility, not slope.
- **Financing scales with the net cash, not the index.** On a flat curve,
  `financing(K) = (L − K)/L × financing(0)` to machine precision, K = 0 through 39.
- **The strike decides which end of the window a buyer works.** Deterministic mean exercise
  day **359.5** struck at 30, **4.5** struck at 50, on the same 365-day window. A payer
  defers, a receiver accelerates.
- **A swing is an obligation, not an option on the strike.** `dV/dK` is exactly the fixed
  volume at every strike at 0 %, no kink at the money, straight to 1.6e-16. At 10 % it rises
  8,761 → 8,891 — 0.4 % of real convexity, the schedule responding to the strike rather than
  a payoff kink.
- **`delta / physical = E[S | exercise] / F`.** On the reference call swing: 1.49 in January
  (picky — exercise only on a spike, transacting at 59.67 against a 40 forward), 0.76 in
  December (forced by the quota, transacting at 30.21), 1.109 for the book.
- **Everything money-denominated is a PV at `valDate`.** Moving the valuation date rescales
  the deterministic value by `exp(−r·ΔT)` to **3.6e-16**. Four exceptions, all labelled where
  reported — see the discounting section of [MODEL-CONVENTIONS.md](MODEL-CONVENTIONS.md).
- **Flat-curve invariants.** Intrinsic exactly 0 without a rate; extrinsic exactly 0 without
  vol, with exercise then at day 359.5 of 365; a deal forced to take every day pays exactly
  the level; price rises monotonically with size.

## Process notes

Three things bit during the day and are worth not repeating.

- **`git add -A` is not safe in this repository.** It swept the user's mid-edit `ttf q.xlsx`
  into a commit (caught before pushing and amended out), and on an earlier day committed an
  Excel lock file. Stage explicit paths; `~$*` is now in `.gitignore`.
- **`jupyter nbconvert --execute` reported a `SyntaxError` in a cell and still exited 0.** A
  failed run looked like a successful one, and the notebook kept its previous outputs while
  its source had moved on. Every code cell is now compiled before being written.
- **Two remotes point at the same repository** — `origin` on the pre-rename URL
  (`dmitrygoryunov2000`), `publish` on the current one (`dmitry-goryunov`). GitHub's
  `This repository moved` line on every push was the tell, and it was filtered as noise for
  most of the session. It surfaced when `git branch -d` refused a fully-merged branch because
  its upstream was the *other* remote's stale ref. There is also a stray
  `refs/remotes/target/main` belonging to no configured remote.

One more, not a code defect: `ttf q.xlsx` was edited outside the session — one row flattened
to 40.00 for a sense check — which silently changed every valuation reading that file, and it
is the only copy of the quote matrix. `CURVE_SOURCE = "flat"` now does the same sense check
against a synthetic curve and touches no data.

## What this leaves open

- **The strike/timing coupling is a settlement-convention artefact.** The fixed leg settles
  on the exercise day, same as the index. A contract paying it monthly, or on any other
  schedule, would not couple them this way. That is roadmap **P1.3**, still open along with
  where a production discount curve comes from.
- **A hurdle rate is not a funding rate.** Discounting a hedgeable commodity flow at a 10 %
  internal project rate is a capital-budgeting view, not a mark-to-market, and it
  systematically favours early exercise relative to a hedged book. Both are legitimate;
  record which one a given valuation used.
- **Calibration is untouched.** `sVol` still defaults to 0.9 and `sMR` to 1.0 with no
  recorded estimation window, method or as-of date. This remains the largest gap and the one
  thing none of today's work moved.
- The rest of [`.planning/ROADMAP.md`](../.planning/ROADMAP.md) — P1.1, P1.2, P1.4, P2.1,
  P2.2, P3.4 — is unchanged.
